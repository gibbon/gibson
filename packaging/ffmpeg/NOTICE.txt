FFmpeg
======

This download includes a copy of FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), which Gibson runs
to decode video and sound. FFmpeg is a separate program, not part of Gibson.

- Version: FFmpeg 9.1, unmodified source, built as a slim decode-only program
  (see packaging/ffmpeg/build.sh in Gibson's repository for the exact options).
- Licence: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later. The licence
  text is included (COPYING.LGPLv2.1).
- Source code: the exact source used (ffmpeg-9.1.tar.xz) is published with each
  Gibson release, next to these downloads. It is also available from
  https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.1.tar.xz
- You may replace this ffmpeg with any other build: put it in the same place
  (next to gibson.exe, or inside Gibson.app/Contents/MacOS), or delete it to use
  the one installed on your system instead.
